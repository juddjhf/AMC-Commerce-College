Who We Are

A trusted institution committed to academic excellence.

AMC Commerce College is one of the leading commerce colleges dedicated to providing high-quality education in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Taxation and Business Management.


Since its establishment, the college has been committed to developing knowledgeable, ethical and industry-ready graduates through experienced faculty, practical learning and modern teaching methods.

Our Mission

To provide quality commerce education that develops leadership, analytical thinking, ethical values and professional skills required for global careers.

Our Vision

To become a centre of excellence in commerce education, producing competent professionals who contribute positively to society and the business world.
