Income Tax

B.Com Course - Income Tax Law & Practice

Introduction to Income Tax

Income Tax is a direct tax imposed by the government on the income earned by individuals, businesses, and other entities. It is one of the major sources of revenue for the government. The collected tax is used for development activities, infrastructure, education, healthcare, and public services.

Meaning of Income Tax

Income Tax means the tax charged on the total income earned by a person during a financial year. Every taxpayer is required to calculate their taxable income and pay tax according to the applicable tax rates.

Objectives of Income Tax

  • To generate revenue for government expenses.
  • To reduce economic inequality among people.
  • To encourage savings and investments.
  • To support national development programs.
  • To maintain economic stability.

Heads of Income

According to Income Tax rules, income is divided into five main heads:

1. Income from Salary

Income received by an employee from an employer is considered salary income.

2. Income from House Property

Income earned from renting or owning house property comes under this head.

3. Business or Profession

Profit earned from business activities or professional services is taxable under this head.

4. Capital Gains

Profit received from selling capital assets like property, shares, etc. is called capital gain.

5. Other Sources

Income such as interest, gifts, and other earnings are included in this category.

Importance of Income Tax

Income Tax plays an important role in the economic development of a country. It helps the government to provide better facilities and maintain public welfare programs.

Income Tax Return (ITR)

Income Tax Return is a form submitted by taxpayers to declare their income, deductions, and tax liability to the Income Tax Department. Filing ITR helps maintain financial records and claim eligible refunds.

Conclusion

Income Tax is an important part of the taxation system. Understanding income tax helps B.Com students learn taxation principles and financial management effectively.
