Financial Accounting

Learn the language of business through Financial Accounting. This course provides students with a strong foundation in accounting principles, financial reporting, bookkeeping, accounting standards and practical business applications.

Explore Course

Course Overview

Financial Accounting is one of the most important subjects in the Bachelor of Commerce program. Every organization needs accurate accounting records to understand its financial position and make business decisions.

Students will learn accounting concepts, journal entries, ledger posting, trial balance preparation, final accounts, depreciation, inventory valuation, bank reconciliation, financial statements and accounting standards.

The course combines theoretical concepts with practical accounting software training, allowing students to become industry-ready professionals.
